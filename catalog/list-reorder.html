Vertical ordered list

Press Enter or Space on a reorder button to pick an item up. Use Up, Down, Home, or End to focus a destination. Enter or Space drops it at that position; Escape cancels. Tab or Shift+Tab cancels and moves focus normally. You can also drag or use the previous/next buttons.
